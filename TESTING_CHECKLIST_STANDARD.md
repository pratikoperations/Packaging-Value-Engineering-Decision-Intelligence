# PVE 1.1 Testing Checklist Standard

## Rule

Testing checklists are configuration-driven by packaging category and later filtered by objective and change type. A checklist identifies required validation activity; it does not certify technical feasibility.

## Category checklists

- Corrugated: GSM, moisture, ECT, BCT, burst, drop, vibration, stacking, route trial.
- Folding carton: GSM, caliper, stiffness, crease, compression, rub resistance, print quality, packing-line trial.
- Rigid plastic: weight, capacity, top load, drop, leak, torque, dimensional inspection, product compatibility.
- Flexible packaging: thickness, OTR, WVTR, seal strength, bond strength, COF, migration, pouch burst/drop, shelf-life validation.
- Labels: adhesion, peel, tack, temperature resistance, print durability, application-line trial, migration.
- Closures: torque, leakage, opening force, thread fit, tamper evidence, drop, product compatibility.
- Glass: impact, pressure, thermal shock, dimensional tolerance, capacity, product compatibility, transport trial.
- Metal: gauge, seam integrity, burst pressure, corrosion, coating integrity, migration, transport trial.

## Checklist metadata

Each generated item must support category, objective, change type, baseline/proposed designation, requirement status, source classification, evidence reference, test date, supplier/laboratory, result status, reviewer comment, and criticality.

## Criticality

Critical items can block progression regardless of percentage completeness. Examples include compression evidence, leak and product compatibility, migration compliance, barrier evidence, closure-thread fit, pressure/thermal shock, and seam integrity.

## Governance

- Engineering validation remains mandatory.
- Human approval remains mandatory.
- Missing critical tests block technical recommendation.
- Supplier-declared results are not marked tested.
- A checklist completion percentage cannot approve a design.
