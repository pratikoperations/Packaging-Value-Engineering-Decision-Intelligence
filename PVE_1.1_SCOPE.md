# PVE 1.1 Scope

## In Scope
Eight packaging categories: corrugated, folding cartons, rigid plastic, flexible packaging, labels, closures, glass, and metal.

Each category supports project intake, objective and change-type selection, input/document guidance, Excel template generation, structured Excel upload, validation, readiness scoring, blockers, available/unavailable outputs, common commercial calculations, testing checklists, and executive reporting.

## Explicit Limit
PVE 1.1 is an intake and validation-readiness platform. It does not provide full engineering feasibility for all packaging categories.

## Out of Scope
OCR, PDF/Word/image extraction, AI document reading, authentication, supplier portal, ranking or allocation, negotiation, notifications, cloud database, ERP, Power BI, ML, live pricing, autonomous approval, deep all-category engineering calculations, and advanced corrugated ECT/BCT prediction.
