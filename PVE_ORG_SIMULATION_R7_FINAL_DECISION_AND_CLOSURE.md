## Final recommendation

# DECISION DEFERRED

Reason:

The software/governance workflow is credible and the synthetic organizational process is internally coherent, but the evidence required to change the decision to GO or CONDITIONAL GO is intentionally absent.

A future real organizational pilot requires:
- a named real sponsor;
- real procurement ownership;
- real packaging-engineering review;
- real Finance/value review;
- approved data ownership;
- real UAT participants;
- observed manual baseline;
- observed PVE-assisted run.

## Enterprise decision

The current public Streamlit application remains a governed portfolio/reference system.

It must not be treated as:
- an approved enterprise production application;
- a confidential-data environment;
- an ERP/PLM/SRM integration endpoint;
- an autonomous approval engine.
