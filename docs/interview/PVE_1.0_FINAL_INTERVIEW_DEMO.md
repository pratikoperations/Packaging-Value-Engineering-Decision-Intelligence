# PVE 1.0 Final Interview Demonstration

## Positioning
“I built a controlled packaging value-engineering decision system that links validated data, configurable business thresholds, deterministic scenario analysis, mandatory engineering controls, and immutable decision history.”

## Seven-Minute Demonstration

### 1. Project Portfolio
Open the Project Dashboard and show multiple projects, explicit active-workspace selection, metadata-only duplication, and archived-project protection.

### 2. Controlled Data Ingestion
Open Upload and Validation. Show canonical JSON, limited CSV templates, field-level issues, duplicate detection, and immutable dataset versions.

### 3. Business Thresholds
Open Configurable Business Thresholds. Show the read-only controlled default, project-specific versions, and fixed engineering controls that cannot be disabled.

### 4. Scenario Execution
Open Controlled Scenario Execution. Select exact dataset and threshold versions, enter explicit bounded assumptions, and run the existing deterministic scenario, technical-qualification, and risk engines.

### 5. Explainable Controls
Explain that critical risk and not-qualified alternatives are blocked, while insufficient evidence cannot become eligible. Business thresholds never override engineering validation.

### 6. Decision Snapshot
Open Decision Snapshot and History. Prepare a snapshot from a saved scenario and show the preserved project, dataset, threshold, and scenario references.

### 7. Decision History
Save the immutable snapshot and inspect technical, risk, business-threshold, and mandatory-control evidence in the project-scoped history view.

## Interview Talking Points
- JSON is canonical; CSV is intentionally constrained.
- SQLite is demonstration persistence behind repository interfaces.
- Datasets, thresholds, scenarios, and decisions are immutable.
- The analytical engines are deterministic and testable.
- Engineering validation and human approval remain mandatory.
- Recommendation statuses mean “review readiness,” not approval.
- The system does not rank suppliers, allocate business, or autonomously approve packaging.

## Business Value
- Faster scenario comparison
- Better traceability of assumptions and decisions
- Reduced spreadsheet fragmentation
- Stronger governance and evidence retention
- Clear separation of commercial thresholds from engineering controls

## Procurement and Packaging Relevance
The workflow supports category management, value engineering, should-cost discussions, packaging optimization, risk review, supplier negotiations, and approval preparation without replacing packaging engineers or authorized decision makers.
