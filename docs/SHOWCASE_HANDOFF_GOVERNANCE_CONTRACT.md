# Showcase and Handoff Governance Contract

## Purpose

The Showcase and Handoff Hub is a deterministic, read-only presentation-orchestration layer for interview demonstrations, executive reviews and new-user handoffs. It guides users to existing governed pages and does not calculate, persist, approve or modify any business record.

## Controlled journeys

The hub provides static journeys for a five-minute executive interview, a ten-minute detailed demonstration, procurement leadership, packaging technical review, technical governance review and new-user handoff. Journey identifiers, step order, page references, durations, proof statements, limitation statements and recovery instructions are immutable.

## Duration and navigation

The five-minute route is limited to 330 seconds and six page transitions. It begins with business context and synthetic-data disclosure and closes with governance boundaries. No journey performs automated navigation or browser control.

## Separation of responsibilities

SourceMate explains why a governed status or outcome exists. Calculation Evidence shows how a supported stored numeric result was constructed. The Decision Evidence Ledger shows chronology and record relationships. The hub only points to those controlled capabilities and does not duplicate their logic or content.

## Claim boundaries

Potential savings are not realized savings. A recommendation is not approval. Review eligibility is not engineering release. A decision record is not supplier award. Consumption authorization is not engine execution. Synthetic demonstration data is not validated supplier, laboratory, production or commercial data.

## Handoff boundary

Handoff readiness means the application can be started, demonstrated, tested and explained reproducibly. It does not establish enterprise production readiness, authentication, authorization, integration, security certification, value-realization tracking or operational support.

## Determinism and export

Identical static journey definitions produce identical Markdown and canonical JSON. Exports contain no generated timestamp and do not persist user progress or acknowledgement.

## Prohibited capability

No analytical-engine change, formula change, threshold change, recommendation change, database table, migration, business-record creation, persistent checklist, telemetry, browser automation, unrestricted prompt, LLM-generated script, PDF, PowerPoint, video, authentication, role management, deployment, release or tag is included.

## Human control

The presenter chooses the journey and controls the demonstration. Engineering validation, procurement review, commercial approval and supplier decisions remain human responsibilities.

## Known governance exception

The pre-existing stale Build 1 wording remains unchanged under explicit authorization. Build 5 does not correct or reinterpret it.
