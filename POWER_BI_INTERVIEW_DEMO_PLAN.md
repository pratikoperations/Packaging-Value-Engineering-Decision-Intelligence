# Power BI Interview Demo Plan

## Objective
Demonstrate how PVE operational evidence becomes executive procurement intelligence without replacing the Streamlit workflow.

## Seven-Minute Demo
1. **Business problem — 45 sec**
   Explain fragmented packaging-cost, qualification, risk, and decision evidence.
2. **Operational workflow — 60 sec**
   Show Streamlit as the controlled layer for project setup, scenario execution, and immutable decisions.
3. **Architecture — 45 sec**
   Explain the shared SQL source and read-only Power BI reporting layer.
4. **Executive overview — 60 sec**
   Show savings, qualification, critical risk, and recommendation-for-review KPIs.
5. **Alternative and scenario analysis — 75 sec**
   Drill into cost, technical status, risk, assumptions, and thresholds.
6. **Decision history and controls — 75 sec**
   Show exact project, scenario, dataset, and threshold references; explain immutability and human approval.
7. **Business impact and limitations — 60 sec**
   Summarize productivity, traceability, decision quality, synthetic-data boundaries, and deferred productionization.

## Core Positioning
“The Streamlit application executes controlled procurement and packaging workflows. The shared SQL reporting layer makes those governed outputs available to Power BI for executive analysis. Power BI does not approve decisions or replace the operational system.”

## Acceptance Criteria
- complete within seven minutes
- show one savings insight and one blocked-risk case
- show source traceability
- state that engineering validation and human approval remain mandatory
- avoid claims of ERP integration, autonomous approval, or enterprise production readiness
