# Category Registry

The registry is configuration-driven and contains eight categories:

- corrugated
- folding_carton
- rigid_plastic
- flexible_packaging
- labels
- closures
- glass
- metal

Every category definition must provide objectives, category-specific change types, fields, documents, units, ranges, tests, blockers, warnings, and available/unavailable analyses. Streamlit pages consume registry services and must not duplicate category logic.
