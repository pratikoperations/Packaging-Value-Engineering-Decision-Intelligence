## Governance boundaries

PVE 1.3 is released as a governance-closed reference implementation. This release does not certify or authorize production deployment, enterprise security, enterprise production readiness, live integrations, real-user pilot acceptance, autonomous engineering approval, supplier ranking, preferred-supplier recommendation, sourcing award, allocation or commercial-term approval.

## Known limitations and exclusions

PVE 1.3 does not include automatic DXF geometry or dimension extraction, cut/crease/slot recognition, parametric dieline generation, automated blank optimization, full CAD editing, 3D folding, tooling design, manufacturing-ready drawing generation or approval, autonomous engineering approval, or ungoverned production deployment.

## Recommended use

Use PVE 1.3 for controlled demonstrations, architecture review, governance validation, procurement and packaging workflow evaluation, and preparation for a separately authorized pilot-readiness phase.

- the proposed tag already exists at another commit;

- a new unresolved blocker is identified;
- release notes introduce production, security, deployment or pilot claims;
- the publication scope expands beyond the governance-closed reference implementation.
