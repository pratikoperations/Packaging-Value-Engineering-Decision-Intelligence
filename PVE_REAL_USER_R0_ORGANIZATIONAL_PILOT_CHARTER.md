## Purpose

Validate PVE with actual observed user evidence and properly governed organizational roles before any enterprise implementation or production decision.

The phase is bounded to one corrugated Existing-versus-Proposed packaging value-engineering case.

## Pilot decision hypothesis

A later GO or CONDITIONAL GO is available only if observed organizational evidence demonstrates that PVE:
1. reduces actual decision-preparation effort;
2. improves evidence traceability and completeness visibility;
3. surfaces technical blockers without overstating approval;
4. preserves packaging-engineering and procurement authority;
5. maintains project/evidence isolation;
6. produces useful review-ready outputs;
7. can operate within approved organizational data/security boundaries.

## Bounded use case

**Category:** corrugated secondary shipping case / RSC

**Decision:** Existing approved specification versus proposed lightweighted/revised-paper specification.

The pilot may use:
- approved real evidence;
- approved sanitized evidence;
- controlled synthetic evidence only where clearly identified and not substituted for required real measurements.

## Evidence requirements

Real-user pilot evidence must distinguish:
- observed human measurements;
- system-generated deterministic evidence;
- organizational approvals;
- sanitized/real source data;
- synthetic controls.

Synthetic assumptions may not be promoted to:
- PILOT-MEASURED;
- FINANCE-VALIDATED;
- realized savings;
- organizational UAT acceptance.

## Protected controls

Preserve:
- deterministic calculations;
- all 25 governed specification fields;
- source provenance;
- human review/correction;
- engineering approval boundary;
- commercial/risk logic;
- immutable history;
- project isolation;
- read-only exports;
- exact-head CI;
- rollback.

## Explicitly prohibited in this phase unless separately authorized

- production deployment;
- enterprise architecture implementation;
- live ERP/PLM/SRM integration;
- OCR;
- external AI runtime;
- autonomous engineering approval;
- supplier award/allocation;
- confidential data without explicit data-owner approval.

## R0 exit gate

R0 is complete when:
- authoritative baseline is reconciled;
- no unresolved open PR/issue blocks the phase;
- pilot charter exists;
- required role matrix exists;
- evidence-use boundary exists;
- next gate explicitly requires named organizational authority rather than assuming it.

R1 may not complete until the required named roles are supplied.
